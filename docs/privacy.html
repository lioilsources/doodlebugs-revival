Doodlebugs Revival — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026

The short version

Doodlebugs Revival collects no data. None.

What the app does with your network

The game is a local multiplayer dogfight. To find nearby players it uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity on iOS (and local network discovery on other platforms). Everything — player names, scores, game state — travels directly between the devices in the room and is gone when the match ends. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, because there is no server to send it to.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs. (The billboards inside the game are fictional brands, painted into the scenery.)
  • No data collection, storage, or sharing of any kind.

Children

Since the app collects nothing, this applies to players of every age.
