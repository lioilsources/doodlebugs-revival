Doodlebugs Revival — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

The short version

Doodlebugs Revival collects no data. None.

What the app does with your network

The game is a local multiplayer dogfight. To find nearby players it uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity on iOS, a broadcast on the local Wi-Fi network on Android and desktop, and Google's Nearby Connections (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, handled by Google Play services on the device) on Android phones without Wi-Fi. Everything — player names, scores, game state — travels directly between the devices in the room and is gone when the match ends. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, because there is no server to send it to.

Permissions

  • Local Network (iOS) and Nearby devices / Bluetooth (Android): only to find and talk to the other players' devices in the room. Older Android versions call the Bluetooth scan a location permission; the game never reads your location.
  • You can refuse them: the game still runs and you can fly a practice round alone.

On your device

Your settings (controls, theme, sound) are stored on the device only and are deleted with the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No data collection, storage, or sharing of any kind.

Children

Since the app collects nothing, this applies to players of every age.
