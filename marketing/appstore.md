## Description
Grab a friend, sit in the same room, and dogfight.

Doodlebugs Revival is a local-multiplayer biplane arcade shooter. Nearby
iPhones and iPads find each other on their own, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth:
no accounts, no servers, no setup. Launch the game on both, they pair in
seconds, and the sky fills with tracer fire. Alone? Tap PRACTICE and fly
against a training drone.

- FAST ROUNDS: first to 3 kills or the 3-minute bell. Best-of-5 runs with
  a podium for the champion.
- THE HANGAR: before every round, draft a weapon (machine guns, flak,
  bombs, rockets, mines, homing seekers), pick a gadget (nitro, barrel
  roll, thunderclap) and spend run points on shield, hull, engine and
  fire-rate upgrades.
- 15 PLANES, 12 PAINTS: from the classic biplane to a dragon, a wasp and a
  paper plane. Every shape fires its own element - fire, lightning, venom,
  plasma.
- DESTRUCTIBLE GROUND: five arenas from a city skyline to a volcano. Shoot
  the terrain apart tile by tile and hide in the clouds.
- SUPPLY DROPS: shoot a crate out of the sky for a special weapon.
- STALL AND RECOVER: let the engine die, flip, relight it - or double-tap
  the stick and the plane does it for you.
- PICK A LOOK: ten hangar themes, from poster art to arcade neon.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection. Just planes.

Playing together needs a second iPhone or iPad nearby. iPhones and
Android phones do not play with each other.
