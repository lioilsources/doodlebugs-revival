## Description
Grab a friend, sit in the same room, and dogfight.

Doodlebugs Revival is a local-multiplayer WWI biplane arcade shooter.
Two to eight devices find each other automatically — no accounts, no
servers, no setup. Launch the game on both, they pair in seconds, and
the sky fills with tracer fire.

- FAST ROUNDS: first to 3 kills or the 3-minute bell. Best-of-5 runs
  with a podium for the champion.
- THE HANGAR: between rounds, draft a new weapon — machine guns, flak,
  bombs, rockets, mines — and spend run points on shield, hull, engine
  and fire-rate upgrades.
- DESTRUCTIBLE SKYLINE: every round rotates the arena and its wall of
  hand-painted vintage billboards. Shoot the towers apart tile by tile;
  hide your plane behind what's left.
- POWER-UPS: downed planes drop balloons — health, shield, repairs,
  damage boosts, weapon tiers. Catch them before they float away.
- COUCH CO-OP: up to 4 pilots on one desktop, gamepads and keyboard;
  gyro or touch controls on phones and tablets.
- 8-BIT HEART: procedural chiptune soundtrack and sound effects, flat
  pixel art, one-more-round pacing.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection. Just planes.

## App Review notes (paste into the Review Notes field)
Doodlebugs Revival is a LOCAL-multiplayer-only party game — there is no
single-player mode and no online matchmaking. Reviewing it needs TWO iOS
devices in physical proximity:

1. Install on both devices and launch.
2. Accept the Local Network permission prompt on both.
3. The devices pair automatically via MultipeerConnectivity within ~15
   seconds (no account, no configuration).
4. Tap READY on both. The battle starts after a 10-second countdown.

A single device will sit on the "SEARCHING FOR GAME..." screen — that is
expected behaviour, not a hang. Demo video of two devices pairing and
playing: [VIDEO LINK — doplnit]
